Ali Aminian, Hao Sheng.

The book walks through ten GenAI products, from email autocomplete and translation to chatbots, RAG, image and video generation, using one interview framework: clarify the requirements, frame the problem as an ML task, prepare data, choose and train a model, evaluate it, and design the serving system.

These are my own study notes in English, not a copy of the book. Each note keeps the design reasoning that matters in an interview, an original diagram of the system, and the questions I would expect to be asked, with short answer outlines. Read the book for the full explanations and figures.
